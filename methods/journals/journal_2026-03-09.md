# Daily Journal — 9 March 2026

## Objective

Improve irregular tunnel segmentation (5-1) beyond the boundary-based baseline; keep the pipeline GT-free; apply hints from past journals (18 Feb geometric ceiling, radial/depth filter, slot inset).

---

## Context & Starting Point

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Boundary-based segmentation (GT boundaries) | 0.742 mIoU | 7 boundaries per ring, no radial filter |
| Error budget | 14% BG false positives, 0% block→BG | 163k unmapped points are all GT-BG (r < radius_min), correctly pred=0 |

**Background:** Boundary-based segmentation replaced adaptive-cap Voronoi as primary method. The dominant error was groove points (GT background) falling inside ring bands and receiving block labels; block points already had 100% pixel mapping.

---

## Experiments & Results (chronological)

### 1. Radial (depth) filter — r_surface_min

**Idea:** Points with r < r_surface_min that receive a block label are reclassified to background. Block surface sits at larger r (e.g. 3.71–3.84 m); grooves at smaller r.

**Sweep:** r_surface_min ∈ {3.50, 3.55, 3.60, 3.65, 3.70, 3.72, 3.75}. Best at 3.70 → mIoU **0.787** (from 0.742). Finer sweep 3.66–3.73: best **3.71** → **0.790**.

**Result:** 51k–55k points reclassified to background per run. BG IoU 0.437 → 0.574 (3.70) → 0.590 (3.71).

### 2. ring_half_width sweep

**Idea:** Vary X extent of ring band (default W / ring_count / 2 = 182.1 px).

**Sweep:** scale ∈ {0.9, 1.0, 1.1}. Best at **1.0** (default). No gain from narrowing or widening.

**Result:** Kept default ring_half_width.

### 3. Per-ring r_surface_min

**Idea:** Block radial extent may vary by ring. Use per-ring threshold from GT block r distribution (1st percentile − 0.01).

**Implementation:** Added `r_surface_min_per_ring` to segmentation (dict of ring_idx → threshold). Fallback to scalar `r_surface_min` for missing rings.

**Result:** mIoU **0.792** (from 0.790). Per-ring values: rings 0–3 → 3.72, ring 4 → 3.71, ring 5 → 3.70, ring 6 → 3.71.

### 4. Boundary Y shift sensitivity

**Idea:** Check if GT-derived boundaries are slightly off; small global shift might improve.

**Sweep:** Add ±10, ±5, 0 px to all boundary y positions (mod height).

**Result:** 0 px best (0.792). Shifts −10, −5, +5, +10 all worse (0.776–0.780). Boundaries left unchanged.

### 5. Slot inset Y (journal 18 Feb hint)

**Idea:** Journal 18 Feb: asymmetric shrink sy=2 on GT bounding boxes gave +0.012 mIoU. Equivalent in boundary model: inset each slot by a few pixels at both Y edges so boundary strips become background.

**Implementation:** Added `slot_inset_y` (default 0). In `build_boundary_label_map`, each slot uses [start_y + inset, end_y − inset) with wrap handling; slots shorter than 2×inset are skipped.

**Sweep:** slot_inset_y ∈ {0, 2, 4, 5, 6}. Best at **2** → mIoU **0.793**.

**Result:** +0.001 over 0.792; BG IoU 0.590 → 0.592. Parameter added to params and retained.

## Key Insights

1. **Radial filter is the main lever for BG false positives.** The 14% of points that were GT background but received block labels sit at smaller r (grooves). A single global r_surface_min (3.71) recovers most of the gain; per-ring refinement adds ~0.002 mIoU.

2. **Journal 18 Feb shrink (sy=2) translates to slot_inset_y=2.** Small Y inset at slot boundaries reduces groove assignment; larger inset (4–6 px) starts cutting into real block extent and hurts.

3. **Boundary positions are already well aligned.** Global Y shift ±5–10 px did not help; GT-derived boundaries are used as-is.

4. **Pipeline remains GT-free at inference.** Boundaries and r_surface_min (and per-ring) are in parameters; they can be GT reverse-engineered for ceiling tests or come from detection (boundaries_per_ring.json) and BO for new tunnels.

5. **Gap to 0.805 (18 Feb).** That result used full GT per-block XY bounding boxes and distance conflict resolution. Our boundary model uses 7 Y boundaries per ring and one X band per ring; no per-block X. Closing the remaining ~0.012 would likely require per-block or per-instance geometry (e.g. per-block X extent or full per-instance boxes), a larger change.
