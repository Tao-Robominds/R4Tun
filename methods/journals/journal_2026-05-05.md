### Landmark K anchor (Task 4)

K depth signature per tunnel + sliding cross-correlation across held-out
depth maps. Implemented but not wired into `J_reflect` as a hard ranker
because the K signatures aren't sharp enough for some tunnels.

Script: `methods/plans/scripts/landmark_k_anchor.py`.

### Iterative reflection on gravity (Task 6)

Removes template rotations and `ring_offset` jitter from candidate
generation (gravity collapses these axes). Keeps fine `y_frac` drift
and scalar/Hough jitter. One ring (`4-6/r276`) jumped 0.584 → 0.760 via
fine y_frac drift, proving the iterative loop finds genuine
improvements when rotation is collapsed.

Script: `methods/plans/scripts/gravity_iterative_reflection.py`.

## Diagnoses & remaining limits (Task 3)

- **4-4/r212, r217**: `proxy_validation_v1` preprocessing degraded depth
  maps to 8% / 15% valid pixels (vs 76% / 77% in `data/ablation/baseline`).
  Detector is starved of pixels. Fix requires re-running preprocessing
  from raw point cloud through the patched `_ring_enhancing.py` —
  blocked because mixing ablation depth with proxy `pixel_to_point.pkl`
  breaks index mapping.
- **5-6/r284**: dense source but K position predicted at y_frac 0.138
  vs measured 0.102. Iterative `yfrac_drift` candidates couldn't bridge
  this offset.
- **4-3/r170**: direction disambiguation chose forward over reverse;
  oracle says reverse would have helped. Cross-correlation epsilon was
  tuned conservatively (`corr_rev > corr_fwd + 0.02`).
