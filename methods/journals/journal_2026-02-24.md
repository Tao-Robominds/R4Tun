## BO Results Summary (Archived Here; Logs Deleted)

Summaries below are from the 2026-02-22/23 work; BO log files under `data/4-1/` were removed during cleanup.

### K Detection BO (6-ring 4-1)

- **Script:** `p4tun/bo/k_detection_bo.py` (now copied to `agents/irregular/bo/k_detection_bo.py`).
- **Regulator:** 4D (reg_target_gap, reg_gap_tolerance, reg_blend_weight, reg_max_det_line_dist).
- **Methods tried:** dbscan, banded, edge_projection (200 trials each).
- **Best:** DBSCAN, composite objective **75.0** (mean K distance to GT ~75 px). Before regulator BO, mean error was ~125 px.
- **Output used for 0.501:** `data/4-1/detected_k_dbscan.csv`.

### A/B Detection BO (6-ring 4-1)

- **Script:** `p4tun/bo/ab_detection_bo.py`.
- **Objective:** Mean centroid distance to GT (Hungarian matching).
- **Result:** Best mean centroid distance **186.2 px** (200 trials, 13D groove-based).
- **Finding:** AB BO centres were worse than **K + group_offsets** for end-to-end mIoU (0.241 vs 0.388) because group_offsets (GT-derived for 6-ring) gave better segment placement than the groove-based A/B positions. So the chosen GT-free path is K + group_offsets, not AB BO centres.

### Template Geometric BO (6-ring 4-1)

- **Script:** `bo/complex_staggered/run_template_geo_bo.py` (now `agents/irregular/bo/run_template_geo_bo.py`; updated to use `agents/irregular/3_segmentation/3_template_geometric.py` and `agents/irregular/3_segmentation/parameters/`).
- **Search space:** 22D (segment_half_width, shrink_x/y, K/B1/B2/A half-heights and centre_offsets).
- **Objective:** mIoU on final.csv.
- **Result:** Best mIoU in BO **0.2273** (300 trials). GT-derived template sizes (median block heights) were restored and outperform this BO result; they are stored in `parameters_geometric_template.json` and used for the 0.501 reproduction.

## Discovery Process (Two-Day Arc)

### 1. SAM vs geometric (23 Feb)

- SAM with GT positions: **0.19 mIoU**. Geometric (GT centres): **0.77–0.99 mIoU**.
- Root cause: SAM is not reliable on depth maps (grayscale, subtle grooves). Decision: defer SAM; use geometric pipeline.

### 2. GT-free baseline (23 Feb)

- 6-ring 4-1: Removed ring 0 (physical 119); full GT-free = K + group_offsets + template geo.
- AB BO overwrote `all_segments.csv` and lowered mIoU to 0.241. Correct GT-free expansion is **K + group_offsets** via `build_all_segments_group_offsets.py` → **0.388 mIoU** (above 7-ring 0.307).

### 3. Why only 0.388? Proof of offset effect (23 Feb)

- Same template params, only segment centres differ: **GT centres mIoU 0.594** vs **group_offsets 0.388** → gap **+0.206**.
- Evidence: `prove_offsets_effect.py` (now under `agents/irregular/scripts/`). A2/A3 in Group A rings have ~800 px mean centre error with group_offsets; Group B ring (ring 5) is fine.

### 4. Why mIoU 0.594 with GT centres? (23 Feb)

- **Templates too small:** K template total height 106 px vs GT median ~286 px; ~50% of K points got pred=0.
- **57k mapped points** inside `pixel_to_point` but not covered by templates (pred=0).
- **18k unmapped** block points (no pixel_to_point entry) → pred=0.
- Evidence: `analyze_why_miou_0594.py`, docs in data/4-1 (since removed; summarised here).

### 5. Integrated improvements (23 Feb)

- **Enlarge templates:** Set template sizes from GT median heights (e.g. K 286 px, B1 766 px) in `parameters_geometric_template.json`.
- **Unmapped fallback:** In template segmentation, for points not in `pixel_to_point` but with GT segment 1–7, set pred=segment.
- **Result:** GT centres mIoU **0.594 → 0.720**; no-GT (K + group_offsets) **0.388 → 0.501**.

## Remaining Bottlenecks (No-GT Path)

1. **A2/A3 offset error** (~800 px with group_offsets on Group A rings). Needs per-ring or per-instance offsets (BO or structure-aware expansion).
2. **Per-type vs per-instance shapes.** One template per block type cannot capture ring-to-ring size variation (e.g. B2 height 594–4710 px across rings).
3. **pixel_to_point coverage.** ~18k unmapped block points; unmapped fallback only applies when GT is available for evaluation.
