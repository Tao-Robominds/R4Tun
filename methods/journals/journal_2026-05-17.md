## v4 -> v5 Progression Summary

### Why v5 after v4

v4 established the reflection pipeline and exposed three deployment blockers:

1. Ring projection rotational ambiguity (physically misaligned yet plausible
   segmentations).
2. Depth-map quality instability across tunnel families (especially irregular
   T4/T5 conditions).
3. Intrinsic selector/proxy mismatch on difficult rings (oracle gap remained).

v5 addressed these as a data-backed deployment engineering cycle:

- enforce depth contract and gravity-referenced stabilisation,
- build broader candidate pools with explicit failure logging,
- retrain intrinsic proxy from observed v5 evidence,
- validate on the 50-ring held-out panel with stagewise scoreboards.

# Addendum (logged 2026-05-29): A/B-offset capability validated -> agents frozen

## Why this addendum
Before rerunning the full experiment, we needed to confirm the `agents/`
pipeline (`1_preprocessing -> 2_detection -> 3_segmentation`) can get close to
each ring's GT ceiling once **K-position AND A/B offsets** are searchable. The
prior workflow only searched K-position; A/B offsets were a fixed geometric
template (`KB7_OFFSETS`), `rotation_shift`/`branch` only relabel/reverse order,
so segment widths were locked.

## Single-instance gate (1-1/r18) — PASSED
Runner: `bo/validation/run_aboffset_validation_1_1_r18_v1.py`.
Evidence: `logs/aboffset_capability_v1/single_instance_gate.json`.

- Agents GT ceiling (GT-optimal K + offsets through the real det+seg): `0.8821`
  (threshold `>= 0.85`).
- BO over (k_y + A/B offsets) from a geometric prior (NOT GT): best `0.8230`.
- Regret vs ceiling: `0.0591` (threshold `<= 0.10`). Gate `passed = true`.
- BO convergence: geometric prior `0.1217` -> random `0.4502` -> BO `0.8230`
  (`logs/aboffset_capability_v1/convergence.csv`).

## Key finding — no agents code change was needed
- The agents already accept arbitrary `k_y_positions` + `per_ring_offsets`
  (`expand_k_with_per_ring_offsets`), so making A/B offsets searchable lives in
  the BO layer, not in `agents/`.
- The only cap below 0.85 was BG/groove gaps between segments being absorbed into
  a block (A2 IoU 0.36, over-predicted 3x). This was removed by engaging the
  agents' EXISTING `r_surface_min` knob at ~2.74 (block surface clusters tightly
  at r~2.745-2.760; ~85% of BG points fall below; threshold is the bimodal
  r-histogram valley, intrinsic / no GT). Ceiling 0.81 -> 0.88, A2 0.36 -> 0.73,
  BG IoU 0.74 -> 0.85.

## Decision — freeze the agents
The `agents/` pipeline is confirmed capable. We therefore **fix (freeze) the
agents now**: no further edits to `agents/**` code. All future capability gains
come from parameters (incl. searchable `per_ring_offsets`, `k_y_positions`,
`r_surface_min`) and from BO/sandbox runners under `logs/`. This is added as a
hard project rule (`AGENTS-IMMUTABILITY`) in
`.cursor/rules/intrinsic-project.mdc`.
