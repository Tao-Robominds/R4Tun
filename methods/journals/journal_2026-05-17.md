## v4 -> v5 Progression Summary

### Why v5 after v4

v4 established the reflection pipeline and exposed three deployment blockers:

1. Ring projection rotational ambiguity (physically misaligned yet plausible
   segmentations).
2. Depth-map quality instability across tunnel families (especially irregular
   T4/T5 conditions).
3. Intrinsic selector/proxy mismatch on difficult rings (oracle gap remained).

v5 addressed these as a data-backed deployment engineering cycle:

- enforce depth contract and gravity-referenced stabilisation,
- build broader candidate pools with explicit failure logging,
- retrain intrinsic proxy from observed v5 evidence,
- validate on the 50-ring held-out panel with stagewise scoreboards.

## Self-Refinement Outcomes

Stage means on 50 rings:

- static SAM4Tun: `0.1374`
- Intrinsics4Tun seeded: `0.1668`
- Intrinsics4Tun stabilised: `0.2901`
- Intrinsics4Tun refined: `0.4750`

Overall improvement:

- `0.4750 / 0.1374 = 3.46x` (`+246%` relative improvement).

Per-tunnel refined-vs-static lift (paired, ring-level):

- T1: lift `+0.2480`, p=`0.0133`
- T2: lift `+0.1928`, p=`0.0479`
- T3: lift `+0.5349`, p=`1.53e-5` (n=9 due one static missing value)
- T4: lift `+0.4187`, p=`0.00110`
- T5: lift `+0.3528`, p=`5.62e-4`

Refinement regressions:

- 12/50 rings where refined < stabilised.
- Concentrated in T4/T5 (irregular/staggered conditions), supporting the
  limitation that proxy ranking remains imperfect under the hardest geometry.
