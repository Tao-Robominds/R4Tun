\noindent\textbf{Evidence summary.} T1/T2 family means (0.591 / 0.652) did not improve when re-tuning the original 18 scalars; L0/S0 remains optimal. T3 panel mean rose from 0.251 (broken vendor) to 0.507 (per-tunnel optimized) via the \emph{new} segmenting and SAM rows above. Oracle \texttt{oracle\_swap} on \texttt{3-1-3} reaches mIoU\,=\,0.829, confirming residual error is block assignment rather than upstream preprocessing.
