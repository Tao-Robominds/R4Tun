# Daily Journal — 23 February 2026

## Objective

Test whether the best GT-tuned geometric segmentation parameters (trial 152, achieved 0.774 mIoU with GT segments) can improve performance when used with non-GT K positions (BO K and Multisource K).

---

## Context & Starting Point

**Baseline:** BO K positions + Current geometric params = **0.305 mIoU** (best non-GT result)

**Previous work:**
- Multisource K Y detection achieved mean |dY| = 900px (vs BO K's 962px), but end-to-end mIoU was worse (0.217 vs 0.305)
- Trial 152 geometric params achieved 0.774 mIoU with GT segments (best from GT-based segmentation BO)
- Hypothesis: Trial 152 params might better tolerate K Y errors and improve Multisource K performance

---

## Experiments & Results

### 1. Test Trial 152 Geo Params with Multisource K

**Setup:**
- K positions: Multisource detection (mean |dY| = 900px to GT)
- Geometric params: Trial 152 (optimized for GT-quality positions)
- Segments file: `all_segments_multisource_locked.csv`

**Result:** mIoU = **0.254** (+17.1% vs Multisource K + Current Geo)

**Analysis:**
- Improvement from 0.217 → 0.254 confirms trial 152 params help when K positions are near-perfect (rings 1, 2, 3 have <20px error)
- Still below baseline (0.305) due to 3 bad rings (0, 4, 6 with >1600px error)

### 2. Test Trial 152 Geo Params with BO K

**Setup:**
- K positions: BO detection (mean |dY| = 962px to GT)
- Geometric params: Trial 152
- Segments file: `all_segments_bo.csv`

**Result:** mIoU = **0.100** (-67.1% vs baseline)

**Analysis:**
- Trial 152 params were optimized for GT-quality positions and don't work with BO K's uniform bias pattern
- Current geo params are better suited for BO K's consistent error distribution

---

## Key Insights

1. **Baseline remains best:** BO K + Current Geo (0.305 mIoU) is still the best non-GT solution
2. **Trial 152 params are position-dependent:** They help Multisource K (which has 3 near-perfect rings) but hurt BO K (uniform bias)
3. **Detection is the bottleneck:** Multisource K has 3 rings (0, 4, 6) with >1600px error that segmentation cannot fix:
   - Ring 0: 2360px error (GT Y=1162, MS Y=3522)
   - Ring 4: 1665px error (GT Y=2839, MS Y=1175)
   - Ring 6: 1710px error (GT Y=1865, MS Y=3575)
4. **Geometric segmentation cannot compensate for large K Y errors:** Even with best geo params, Multisource K only reaches 0.254 vs 0.305 baseline

---

## Conclusion

**Baseline confirmed as best:** BO K + Current Geo params (0.305 mIoU) remains the optimal non-GT solution.

**Next steps:** Focus detection improvement on rings 0, 4, 6 where Multisource K has >1600px error. These are likely wrap-around or edge cases that the multisource method struggles with. Geometric segmentation cannot compensate for such large K Y errors.
