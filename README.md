## Reasoning for Tunnels

R4Tun: a reasoning-based multi-agent framework for segmental tunnel analysis in point clouds.

Automated inspection of segmental tunnel linings is vital for structural health assessment and long-term operational safety. Modern laser scanning produces large point-cloud datasets of linear infrastructure with highly repetitive lining patterns, but reliably segmenting these into individual components remains a challenging step for downstream deformation analysis and defect localisation. Current pipelines depend heavily on expert-driven hard-coded features, annotated datasets, or specifically tuned parameters,  undermining their suitability for field deployment at scale. This paper presents \textbf{R4Tun}, a reasoning-based multi-agent framework that wraps an existing expert-designed segmentation pipeline with constrained large language models (LLMs) driven reasoning layers for context-aware parameter adaptation. Evaluated on the open-source Seg2Tunnel benchmark under controlled deviation from an expert-tuned reference, R4Tun improves average mIoU from 28.5\% to 44.8\% (\textbf{+}57\% relative), and in off-reference cases prevents baseline collapse from average mIoU 3.8\% to 34.9\% (\textbf{+}818\% relative). The results show that R4Tun exhibits greater robustness by adapting parameters explicitly, as tunnels drift from the expert-tuned reference conditions. The proposed reasoning-based framework demonstrates the potential of multi-agent AI systems to act as transparent, engineer-guided systems, supporting scalable and deployable automation for tunnel lining inspection.
